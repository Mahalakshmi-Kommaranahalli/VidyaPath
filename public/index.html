Aadhaar-NPCI Bank Seeding
#1 Reason for Scholarship Failure

Having your Aadhaar linked to a bank is not enough. Your account must be active on the NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) Aadhaar Mapper for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

How to verify: Visit resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper or dial *99*99*1# on your registered mobile to check NPCI status. If inactive, visit your bank branch and submit the "Aadhaar NPCI Mandate Form".

Frequently Asked Questions
Quick Solutions & Common Queries
Q: Can I receive both government and private CSR scholarships?
Generally yes, private CSR grants (like Reliance or HDFC) can supplement government scholarships unless the specific government notification explicitly restricts dual benefits.
Q: How do I obtain a digital Income / Caste certificate?
Apply via your state's online Seva Sindhu / MeeSeva / e-District portal or at your local Gram Panchayat Common Service Center (CSC). Ensure it contains a verifiable barcode.
Q: What if I have 58% marks and cutoff is 60%?
Check our "Near Matches" tab. Many state government and private trust schemes relax criteria for SC/ST/PwD applicants or evaluate holistic need.
